Company Remote Work Policy (Effective Jan 2026)

Eligibility:
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period are eligible to work remotely up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval.

Equipment:
The company provides a laptop and a one-time $300 home office stipend for eligible remote employees. Requests for the stipend must be submitted through the IT portal within 60 days of eligibility.

Core Hours:
Remote employees must be available between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in their local time zone for meetings and collaboration.

Expense Reimbursement:
Internet reimbursement of up to $50/month is available for employees who work remotely more than 2 days per week. Submit receipts via the Expenses tab in the HR portal.
